other of the Sophist.”” When Plato descends from the transcen-

they are necessary conditions of intelligible speech, present them-
selves as well among the ideas as in concrete things, and the
θάτερον is then as truly an οὐσία or φύσις (Sophist 258 B) as the
ταὐτόν.
