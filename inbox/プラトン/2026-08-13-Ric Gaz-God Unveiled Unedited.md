The Bible

The Bible is “little books” in simple terms derived from the Greek word “biblia”.
It states a lot of laws but is neither a law book nor an accuser. It is the way to life
where every stroke of the pen is about God’s love and who we are to Him.

The Bible came from many books written over thousands of years, chosen,
compiled and canonized by council into what is called Biblia. The Old Testaments
were canonized before the time of Jesus Christ, into what is known as Jewish canon.
New Testaments were written accounts of Jesus, and with council agreeing on the
Jewish canon, were compiled to become the Biblical canon. Some books generally
not included in the canon are Apocrypha, Pseudepigrapha and Gnostic which were
discounted due to vagueness of authenticity and relevance. Most non canon books
were written in silent years where no prophets were around from the era of Malachi to
John the Baptist and, Revelation to the Day of the Lord. Thereof visions and
prophecies were sealed! up to anoint the Most Holy.

Most of the separate books in the Old Testament canon were written by faithful
people who had feared? God, which brought more weight to the scriptures as true and
relevant, where Jesus quoted from the Jewish canon when He was sharing wisdom to
others. New Testament was written after the end time did not occur, the four main
synoptic gospels contains multiple errors but are not against God or mankind. In
effect, the Biblical canon with both Old and New Testaments reveal the consistency
of God’s nature despite the fact that the Bible was written over thousands of years.

His Nature

The Bible represents God and reveals His Holiness and nature, in so makes the
Bible alive and not just a book. John 1:1 In the beginning was the Word, and the
Word was with God, and the Word was God. The same was in the beginning with
God. All things were made by him; and without him was not any thing made that was
made. In Him is life, and that life is the light of men. The Bible breathes of God and
His Love is written all over it. As the world was weaned in Old Testaments, faith,
righteousness and mercy was imparted. At the appointed time when the world was
ready, the tender loving Lord shared pure innocent love to the world. The purpose of
the Bible is to align the hearts of mankind to likeness of the Lord. Love, Grace,
Mercy and reading the Bible or hearing it enables us to know Him, for His Words
were written and communicated within.

God allowed mankind to write the Bible, throughout thousands of years with many
books in the Bible from so many people, yet amazingly it is consistent of who God is.

God is alive but there is still a need for the Bible, for if we do not know His nature,
He will always be a stranger even while being next to us. God is always half a step
away from mankind, who once appeared in front of Israel in the desert. The sinful
people trembled in fear of His majestic appearance, and begged for Moses to be their
arbitrator instead, fearing they might be consumed by His grandeur because of their
sins. Therefore God promised a Messiah* to come amongst His people in His Name
to save them. The time when He walked the earth amongst mankind, many did not
know Him, where He was innocently crucified. Even after He rose from the tomb His
people who walked side by side with Him couldn’t recognize Him, till He spoke of
things concerning Himself from scriptures in the Bible’.

The Bible was written mostly by faithful people of God on accounts of what
happened. God also wrote in the Bible which was the Ten Commandments and most
of mankind could not uphold it’. The commandments were penned, because mankind
has the tendency to alter or forget the Words through sharing by words of mouth.
Though written, the Bible is meant more to be shared personally and humanely from
generations to generations, parents to children®. For Earth is a special place to be in
where humans are given the power to create, and thus be parents also. As a newborn
comes forth to the world, the words of care, concern, and of love, come verbally from
parents. It is God Himself who vested the divine power to humans to be parents. Not
that God is not personal with everyone or doesn’t want to, but more significantly the
loving relationship of a child and parent is that of God’s; as is with the child and the
creator.

But mankind with fallen nature could not uphold the pure love that their children
are supposed to receive, where there are also times the Bible was not shared with
them. Fortunately there is the Bible where anyone can know and receive the primary
source of pure love. And after knowing the loving nature of God from the scriptures,
whether He is in front of us or not does not matter anymore. If anyone should open
their hearts to Him, there won’t be a need to rely on open eyes to see.

Accuracy of the Scriptures

Jesus held too much similarities to the Old Testaments that one may think He is
conjured from it. But how different should those sent of God be especially a Messiah
and where did John the Baptist, parables and knowledge of Heaven come from?
Neither would worldly writers let their own parables be revealed thousands of years
later’ if they knew the meanings, for it was more beneficial and convincing for them
to reveal their wisdom and gain support in their generation. Even as it is of current,
wouldn’t reading the Old Testaments be understandable of the true nature of God with
love and mercy after the work of salvation was made complete on the cross?

Though writers of the scriptures tried to give the best possible accounts of
whatever happened, there were some errors that were penned in the New Testaments.
For instance, some say Judas hanged®, some say he fell’ to fulfill the scriptures in the
field bought with the betrayal silver. Given the Gospel were mostly written more than
forty years after the cross, this presented arguments of precise words and accuracy of
texts in the Bible. And this continued to confuse and make mankind doubt!” the Bible
which was written by mankind, and failed to see the synthesis behind the analogy.

Whether Judas hanged or fell from the tree, or jumped from a cliff, doesn’t change
the fact he died. Whether the field was bought by the Pharisees or Judas, doesn’t
change the point the field was bought with the betrayal silver. And just because the
world thinks God is of wrath doesn’t change the fact that God has always been loving
and merciful. If God is perfect why did He not correct these inaccuracies? But if He
did, would you have then believed everything else in it? As with God’s nature it is
heartbreaking but fine if you choose not to believe, a lot matters of the heart did He
hid in parables on purpose for a good measure.

Just as the word perfect is frequently misunderstood, saying God is perfect. And
derived God has perfect divine order so divine justice needs to be met and Jesus
fulfilled them. How so then if Abraham, Jacob and David with sins are of perfect
Heaven and why didn’t Jesus come earlier? Jesus came after them so how did David
fulfill His perfect divine order to be of Heaven? God said to wipe out Israel in the
desert, Nineveh and the generation of Jesus, why did it not happen? If someone slaps
us is it not right to slap back, eye for an eye? So mankind who slapped God with
accusations, condemnations and sins, is it not right for Him to slap back. But
mankind fell to the ground and pleaded for mercy and God did not exercise His
rights'' but gave compassion and mercy out of love instead. For God is perfect in
love and righteousness with no wickedness or fault at all which Jesus!” testified. And
perfect love is never rigid where pure love exceeds perfect righteousness.

With Heart Wide Open

It is often argued whether the Bible should be read literally, or figuratively. Those
who argued have probably read the Bible, and know that there are times to read
figuratively'*. But there are so many opinions of humans about how it should be
interpreted, which fortunately God helped in the matter which has come to be known
as “Proof Texting”.

Effectively, reading with an open and pure heart'* will enable the scriptures to be
as clear as water. With self justifications, reasoning and knowledge, who God is and
His Words can hardly be understood if a heart is proud. But any humble heart of love
without reason or knowledge can understand much scripture in the Bible. The Old
Testaments may be a little difficult to understand before Jesus came to reveal the
scriptures, but believing it will allow hearts of love to understand the entire Bible.

Proof Texting
Deuteronomy 19:15 One witness shall not rise up against a man for any iniquity, or

for any sin, in any sin that he sinneth: at the mouth of two witnesses, or at the mouth
of three witnesses, shall the matter be established.

Two or Three Witnesses.

All laws from God are good, thus scholars toil to support their points where they
need two or more verses to support their points and no verses to discriminate it. And
all supporting verses are to be read in context and not just quotes. There must not be
disagreement with any other two or three passages, which then make any points not in
agreement. In context means reading and understanding the entire verse, chapters and
sometimes the entire books. If there are many passages to disprove their points, their
points will less likely be accepted.

Many people like to quote favorite verses, which sometimes are not even the full
sentence in the Bible just to suit the situation or to support their point of view. Yet no
one should just pluck out any words from the Bible for all situations. Faithful and
loving people who are with God’s love understand the verses or passages to quote,
where their tongues will hardly cause any unease of hearts to humble recipients.

Not a Law Book

As mentioned, the Bible is not a law book or accuser but a light and the way to life.
It is all about God’s love and who we are to Him.

God gave all the laws and commandments to humans so that it exposes goodness
and sins of mankind. Sin by means is to fall short of God’s glory and perfection. The
commandments are to bring us closer to God and foster a generation of righteousness
in the world. But by the perfection of the laws mankind could hardly establish full
goodness back and will always be short of God’s glory and perfection. That was why
Jesus came to reveal the truth in the entire scriptures, which is God’s pure love. With
innocent love in the heart, the commandments can be kept without needing to try, and
there is much more joy in it which brings all life to the fullest.

As meant for the Bible to be passed on through words of mouth from generations
to generations, churches with faithful loving servants are always a great help to
experience the love and grace of God. When God spoke from Heaven it was so
magnificent inglorious humans trembled, which so when the Messiah came to Earth
personally and spoke with human tongue the same things, it overflowed with love.
Reading the Bible is a huge difference from hearing the scriptures from humble
loving people with lots of love and grace in their hearts.

Definitely there are many people and churches who claim about being servants of
God but their hearts are far from God’s. A good church is not one that serves itself,
but one that is there for the people. After all humans are still prone to weaknesses so
pray for Jesus to forgive them and move on. There are always many other good
modest churches with many humble servants where ultimately, the Bible is much as
important as churches who shares the messages of God and His Grace personally to
anyone. The walk into eternal life may be ever changing. How the heart changes as
one matures or how it changes with situations one may be in, can pull one away or
nearer to His Words. But one thing for sure is, frequent hearing or reading of the
scriptures makes a lot of difference most times. Of course after knowing when we
should believe!> is the key to all the differences. So as simple as it is, Know, then
have Peace and Faith.

Genesis

In the beginning, with God’s Words the Universe was created. The magnificent
Creation was revealed to mankind that it was done in days. On the seventh day God
was already resting from His Creation’. The Creation was either complete in seven
days, or God did not elaborate the entire Creation but let the world know that He
created the Universe and simplified the phase” of Creation as days. It is not easy to
detail eternity to children, and is not the purpose of the Bible. The apparent thing is
mankind stood on Earth after the entire Universe was completed in its vast arrays.

In the simplest terms Creation was made known to mankind to let us know it is
God who created the Universe, and it is He who will be at the end of the world’. Yet
this is not the most important thing God is conveying to mankind. The most
important thing is having a heart of pure love which consists of the entire Bible. It
was revealed numerous times the true joy of life comes from a pure heart of love, and
thus righteousness.

God of Love

Strange and misfortunate events occur everyday worldwide, and most people
credited God with it. To the world it may seem God who is almighty and powerful is
allowing evil in this world or He may have created evil to test people. Yet the Bible
which tells everything concerning Him reveals to us He did not create evil and
allowing evil to happen to us is not what He intended’.

Genesis 1:1 In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth. And the earth
was without form, and void; and darkness was upon the face of the deep. And the
Spirit of God moved upon the face of the waters. And God said, Let there be light:
and there was light. And God saw the light, that it was good: and God divided the
light from the darkness.

Then God said let there be sky and it became so. Let water be gathered and let dry
ground appear and it was so. And God saw it was good. Let there be trees and plants,
and it was good. Let there be light in the sky for the earth, and it was good. Let there
be living creatures in the waters and sky, and it was good. Let there be living
creatures and livestock on land, and it was good. Then let’s make man in our image
and rule all over the earth and everything in it, and it was good.

God created man and woman, and blessed them to multiply dominating over the
earth and saw it was good’. Thus Heaven and Earth was complete in its vast arrays
and God rested from the works of His Creation.

Everything God created was good, and every smallest detail was made complete
making Earth a great place to be in. The best was given to mankind whom was set
after the entire Universe and Earth was complete to rule over it. This great love was
not much comprehended by Adam and Eve living with total glory without any
complains or groaning. They were given a longer time to comprehend and delight in
love where their lifespan were a thousand years from the beginning. Their hearts and
spirits were supposed to be filled with pure love, as with all generations of mankind.

As with our nature to provide the best for our children,
God gave His best to mankind.

Fall of an Angel

All goodness God had created was sought to be destroyed when evil came. It
began as revealed in the scriptures from an angel who was created full of wisdom and
perfect in beauty, being appointed as the guardian cherub®. A model of perfection till
wickedness found him, and his heart became proud of his beauty as his wisdom
became corrupt by reason of his brightness’. The corruption brought the fallen angel
the name Satan, which means “Adversary”. Satan who once walked the holy mounts
and was in the Garden of Eden faces the punishment set for him, when he withstood
God with wickedness and vileness.

For angels served God and His Creation, where they were to help mankind to His
ways of love. But Satan became proud of himself and the first root of wickedness
came that stood against God. The pride of Satan caused his own downfall where
more wickedness and iniquities found him, and his authority was taken away from
him. God Himself then worked salvation for the world, but Satan with his pride and
wickedness sought to bind the world with wickedness and destroy mankind whom
God loved and was created in the image of God.

While God pardoned Satan time and again, Satan never humbled away from his
pride and wickedness but scheme to bring the world to complete fall and go against
God. The fallen angels became devils and demons where their most vicious attacks
come in silent forms of tempting, deceiving and accusations, leading mankind to fall
from His love and righteousness. Yet the appointed time will come and their
wickedness will find themselves to eternal judgment.

Fall of Mankind

As with a parent to a child, God the Creator loves Adam and Eve. Divinely
vesting power to Adam and Eve to be creators on Earth, it is God’s Will for mankind
to be in the likeness of God. Humans were created in His image and be creators on
Earth. However love, grace and mercy which occasionally bend rules cannot be truly
created, but has to be nurtured to a child to understand and comprehend.

In the Garden of Eden, Adam and Eve lacked nothing where they were given all
that were needed. There were fruits of all kinds for them and God provided the rivers
for the garden that Adam and Eve need not toil for the fruits. While they were
weaned to maturity, God gave a simple command to them which was not to eat from
the tree of knowledge for they would have to die. It was a simple command that
Adam and Eve could understand that though the fruits from the tree of knowledge was
pleasing to their eyes, it will result in their death if taken.

However the wicked one came and tempted Eve to go against God and His
command. With misguiding words spewed from deception, it fed the thoughts to Eve
that God gave false warning about their deaths and was keeping great knowledge
from them. When Eve chose to follow her worldly desires and go against God, her
eyes were opened the moment she took the fruits from the tree of knowledge. Straight
away sin and guilt entered her heart, where she used the same deception to bring
Adam down together with her.

Adam and Eve fell to the same sin devised by wickedness so both directly
disobeyed God when they ate from the tree of knowledge of good and evil®. When
wickedness entered their hearts, they were ashamed and their guilt separated them
from God. With the heavy burden of guilt, they both condemned themselves
immediately and fell to more wickedness to cover their wrongdoings.

From that very moment,
God had to come out from His rest to help mankind for He loves us so.

Adam and Eve who were afraid did not admit their mistakes, nor repent and ask for
mercy. Instead their knowledge spewed more wickedness to start accusing others to
justify themselves and cover their wrongdoings. With this occurrence, God knowing
the cause was from the vile serpent punished it severely, and Eve also got a heavier
sentence than Adam. For where the offences come from, a heavier judgment will be
set. Although grieved, there was no choice but to reprove Adam and Eve for their
sins which made the glory of mankind fell short of God. Sin has its own punishments,
and God would only allow sins to be unpunished through His mercy. Though it is not
the responsibilities of God, He will provide mercy by taking the burdens from
mankind, and help cover the mistakes of mankind with His abundance of goodness.

God who knows the hearts of mankind gives mercy for those who asked with their
hearts, and not their deceitful tongues’. For when a heart asks for mercy, it has
