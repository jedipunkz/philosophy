That the blocking of the Sikia was part of the design in the blockade of Pylos I think there can be no doubt. If any one does doubt the fact, let him remove this factor from the problem, and he will then see the enormous mass of difficulties which the removal would involve. Every mistake or difficulty (and there are many of the latter) in Thucydides' account, with the exception of the length attributed to Sphacteria, is ultimately traceable to his failure to recognise the existence of two harbours.
