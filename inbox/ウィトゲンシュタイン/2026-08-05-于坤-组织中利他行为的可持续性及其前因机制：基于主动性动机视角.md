YU Kun, WANG Zheyuan, PENG Xiongliang, WANG Pei,

Abstract: With the rapid development of the economy and society, the critical role of the sustainability of
altruism is more salient for organizations and their employees to have a better adaptation and greater
prosperity. Although recent studies have found that altruistic behavior is dynamic in nature, there are severe
deficiencies in theoretical frameworks and methods of these studies. They were unable to obtain a
comprehensive picture of the dynamic sustainability of altruistic behavior, not to mention its antecedents
