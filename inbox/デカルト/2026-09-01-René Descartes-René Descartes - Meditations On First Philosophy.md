PREFACE TO THE READER

[1.] I have already a few years ago touched on the questions
of God and the human mind in the Discourse on the Method
of correctly Conducting the Reason and of Investigating the
Truth in the Sciences, published in French in the year 1637,
surely not in order that I might there treat of them accurately,
but only in order that I might offer a sampling of my views and
discern from the judgments of the readers how they were to
be treated of later on. For these questions have seemed to me
to be of such importance that I would judge that with them is
to be dealt more than one time, and I follow a way of explicat-
ing them so little trodden and so remote from the common usage
that I had thought it to be not useful to teach it in more detail
in a writing in French and in one to be read by all people without
distinction, so that the weaker minds could not believe that it
is to be taken by them too.

[2.] But although I have there requested of all those people
to whom there would occur something in my writings worthy

I shall here respond with a few words before I go on to a more
accurate explication of the same things.
