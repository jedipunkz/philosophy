What is the Advantage of Learning Grammar?

Athenian Stranger:: ‘Friends’ we say to them,—‘God, as the old tradition declares,
holding in His hand the beginning, middle, and end of all that is, travels according to
His nature in a straight line towards the accomplishment of His end. Justice always
accompanies Him, and is the punisher of those who fall short of the divine law. To
justice, he who would be happy holds fast, and follows in her company with all
humility and order; but he who is lifted up with pride, or elated by wealth or rank, or
beauty, who is young and foolish, and has a soul hot happy with insolence, and
thinks that he has no need of any guide or ruler, but is able himself to be the guide of
others, he, I say, is left deserted of God; and being thus deserted, he takes to him
others who are like himself, and dances about, throwing all things into confusion,
and many think that he is a great man, but in a short time he pays a penalty which
justice cannot but approve, and is utterly destroyed, and his family and city with him.
Wherefore, seeing that human things are thus ordered, what should a wise man do or
think, or not do or think’?

Clinias:: Every man ought to make up his mind that he will be one of the followers
of God; there can be no doubt of that.

Athenian Stranger:: Then what life is agreeable to God, and becoming in His
followers? One only, expressed once for all in the old saying that ‘like agrees with like,
with measure measure,’ but things which have no measure agree neither with
themselves nor with the things which have. Now God ought to be to us the measure
of all things, and not man, as men commonly say: the words are far more true of Him.

And he who would be dear to God must, as far as is possible, be like Him and such as
He is. Wherefore the temperate man is the friend of God, for he is like Him; and the
intemperate man is unlike Him, and different from Him, and unjust. And the same
applies to other things; and this is the conclusion, which is also the noblest and
truest of all sayings,—that for the good man to offer sacrifice to the Gods, and hold
converse with them by means of prayers and offerings and every kind of service, is the
noblest and best of all things, and also the most conducive to a happy life, and very
fit and meet. But with the bad man, the opposite of this is true: for the bad man has

an impure soul, whereas the good is pure; and from one who is polluted, neither a
good man nor God can without impropriety receive gifts. Wherefore the unholy do
only waste their much service upon the Gods, but when offered by any holy man,
such service is most acceptable to them. This is the mark at which we ought to aim.
Laws by Plato, book 4, Jowett.

If you are not versed in metaphor, the passage reads like simple
mythology: Yet the passage refers to physical and biological fact. Let us

One of the expressions for a thing, a unit, used by some early Greeks
was every thing has a beginning a middle and an end. The segment was
a pure binary expression, stop, go, stop. God is a Universal Intelligible
binary expression. Our every behavior and the behavior of every thing is
also called a thing’s expression. This Universal Binary, is ordered, and
means that the whole of the Universe is ordered. By recognizing that
order we can order our self and our environment in order to do our
biologically defined job, creating standards of life supporting behavior.
God is the fundamental intelligible and fundamental intelligence of the
universe; it allows us to express a life of meaning and intelligence.

Anyone claiming that the Universe is Chaos has committed a self-
referential fallacy and is too stupid to recognize that, if this was true,
there could not even be such a thing as a grammar system to make that
very same claim; every system of grammar is a means of effecting binary
recursion, a result of the very definition of a thing, itself, which is order.

Definition: A thing is a relative constrained by correlatives, or again a
material difference within a shape.

And here is what you will find Plato teaching, and also the Judeo-
Christian Scripture: As the Universe is composed of every thing, it follows
that the Universe is expressible by and as binary recursion; the simplest
being the motion of the hand commensurate with the two elements of a
thing. Thus God is that which expressed the Universe, or again, God
created the heavens and the earth. Every possible system of grammar is
expressed by a particular use of binary recursion, geometry being the
only system of grammar effected by an Arithmetic one-to-one
correspondence between the hand and the intelligible parts of the
Universal and intelligible Binary.

The author of the Bible, however, put this binary into common
grammar as Adam and Eve; Adam and Eve are a Conjugate Binary Pair
which, by complete induction and deduction produces the human race
and just like the metaphor’s of Plato, we find that the Bible brings us
right back to the birth of a mind by binary recursion; Adam and Eve, by
complete induction and deduction, produces all judgments, the final
product of mankind.

A mind, when functional, is the most powerful life support system
possible, it functions when it is fully aware of binary recursion and how
to use it for the recursion of memory to produce life supporting behavior.
Thus, the elements of a thing are a Conjugate Binary Pair which, by
complete induction and deduction produces everything, including all
judgments, or prophesy. We have a single standard, the Universal
Binary, by which to comprehend everything. A mind overwhelmed is not
the universe which overwhelms it.

Metaphor is used to introduce a mind to its biologically defined job, to
encourage one to seek what is good and avoid what is evil and even how
to do that job. Binary recursion affords us four specific categories of
grammar by which to recursively apply to our own memory; Common
Grammar, Arithmetic, Algebra and Geometry. Thus, all of our

experiences in life have a means by which we can understand our
relationship to our environment and plan and execute a productive life.

Our immediate environment is our own body, and beyond that we say
