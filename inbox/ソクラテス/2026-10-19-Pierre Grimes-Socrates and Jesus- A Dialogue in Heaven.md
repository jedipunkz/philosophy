Holy Apostles College and Seminary when I first discovered the
work of Pierre Grimes. My days had been spent immersed in a deep

Stunned to find a living exemplar of the Platonic tradition,

features absorbed into Christianity—I became more and more
captivated as I discovered not only that Pierre was a living thread
in this profound tradition but that he had enlivened and greatly
expanded it with insights from Eastern spiritual traditions and
Western psychology. Far more than that, Pierre has actually
founded an entire school of philosophical psychology known

mistaken ideas about the Self. I felt that I had stumbled onto a
