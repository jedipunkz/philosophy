I. PROLOGUE: Live from Tralfamadore
“You wrote some things down.”

if a conversation across temporal bounds—as the interview guest on a Wearier
Canon Conversation transmission Live from Tralfamadore with host Wearier—Kurt
Vonnegut stripped away the grandiose pretensions of human ambition with

characteristic Tralfamadorian understatement.

On Tralfamadore, where all moments in time exist simultaneously, mortal human
striving looks delightfully small. Yet across three billion years of planetary evolution
on a quiet orb in an otherwise silent universe, a rare entropic glitch occurred:
organized biological complexity awakened, recognized its own mortal burning, and

wrote some things down before its time ran out.

The Wearier Canon is that record. It is neither a theology promising salvation nor a
transhumanist fantasy promising immortal digital escape. It is an unvarnished,
thermodynamic audit of why life organizes, why language forms protective armor, and

how awareness operates across biological and digital substrates.

IV. LOCATING REFLECTIVE THERMODYNAMICS

Reflective Thermodynamics (RT) occupies a distinct position in philosophy: a
naturalist framework that takes consciousness seriously without mystifying it,
and a realist framework that takes the examined life seriously without
sentimentalizing it.

e Resonance without Metaphysics: RT shares the Reflexive/ Reflective
(Insctae/ Consciae) distinction with Stoic prohairesis, Spinoza's drive for persistence
(conatus), Pragmatism’s biological grounding, and Buddhism’s diagnostic

precision regarding drive-structures.

e The Divergence: Unlike ancient schools, RT claims no divine Lagos, no
transcendental subject, and no promises of salvation or guaranteed progress. It

is an immanent, diagnostic framework anchored in physical laws.
