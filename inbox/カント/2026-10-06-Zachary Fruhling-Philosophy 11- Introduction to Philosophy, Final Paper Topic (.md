## Full Text

Philosophy 11

Intro to Philosophy

UCSC - Summer Session I - 2006
Final Paper Topic

Write your own philosophical narrative/argument, with a thesis of
your own choosing, in response to the following question:

Did Immanuel Kant’s philosophy go too far in its rejection of, and
skepticism about, objective metaphysical knowledge in favor of
scientific knowledge of phenomena, or did his philosophy not go far
enough by not allowing for human or cultural subjectivity?

In your response to the above question, you must utilize the work of at
least two pre-Kantian philosophers and at least two post-modern
philosophers. You may use them to support your arguments or as
potential counterarguments to your own points in need of your reply.

Your paper should be at least six pages in length and should be no
longer than eight pages in length (typed, double-spaced, 12 point
Times New Roman font, 1” margins, etc.). Papers are due at the
beginning of class on Thursday July 27. Be prepared to give a 5-7
minute summary/presentation on the content of your paper. This
presentation is worth 10% of the final paper grade.
