## Abstract

Immanuel Kant's 1784 essay is by far the most famous of the responses to Zollner's request for an answer to the question "What is enlightenment?" Dated 30 September 1784, it was written, as Kant explained in afootnote at the close of the essay, without knowledge of the contents of Mendelssohn's response, which appeared in the Berliriische Monatsschrift as Kant was completing his own answer. The essl9' was the second of the fifteen articles Kant wrote for .the Berlinische Monatsschrift in the years between 1784 and 1796.
