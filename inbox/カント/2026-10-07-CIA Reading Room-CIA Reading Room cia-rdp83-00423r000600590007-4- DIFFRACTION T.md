ABSTRACT

A mumber of recent clevelopments inj the theory of diffraction
of electromagnetic waves, particularly those dealing with apertures
in plane conducting screens, are reviewed. The subjects treated
include modifications of Kirchhoff's theory, the theory of small
apertures, Babinet's principle for plane obstacles, variational
principles, and singularities at sharp edges,

For completeness, a discussion from an alternative view-
point of the problem of diffraction by an aperture by Professor

N. Marcuvitz has been included in this report.
