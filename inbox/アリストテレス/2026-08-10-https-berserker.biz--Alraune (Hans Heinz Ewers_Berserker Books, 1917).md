(The story of a strange creature)

Hanns Heinz Ewers was born in Dusseldorf in 1871. He quickly gained a
reputation for his volumes of satirical poetry and his performances as a
member of a travelling theatre company. Between 1907 and 1922, he wrote
several volumes of short stories and major works such as "The Magician's
Wife", "Alraune" (filmed in the 1920s) and "The Vampire". At the height of
his career, he held the post of President of the Fascist Writers' Union under
Hitler. He died in Berlin in 1943.

His fantastic and gruesome stories are very popular
