Once upon a time there was a man who had an old tomcat.
He was so old that he could not catch a mouse. So the man took

Once the Hare met the Vixen and said:

“Vixen, deary, could I come tonight and woo you?”

“Oh no, I have Pan Kotsky to take care of me now, so you
better watch out that he doesn’t tear you to tatters.”
