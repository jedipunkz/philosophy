## Abstract

The textbook was developed within the framework of the academic discipline "Fundamentals of Textology: Methods of Text Analysis and Decoding" and is oriented towards integrative work with the English text of Aristotle's Nicomachean Ethics . It includes analytical reading based on critical thinking techniques, followed by reproduction (retelling) of the content. The book contains assignments and exercises aimed at deep comprehension of the text of the Nicomachean Ethics . 
 
Intended for students, graduate students, and teachers of philological universities and faculties.
