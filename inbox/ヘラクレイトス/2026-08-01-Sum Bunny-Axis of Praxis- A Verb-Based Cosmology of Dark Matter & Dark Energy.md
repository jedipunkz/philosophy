AXIS OF PRAXIS

A Verb-Based Cosmology of Dark Matter & Dark Energy
I. PRELUDE — THE COSMIC TRINITY

We are taught to view the cosmos as a museum. In this mental model, the universe is a vast, cold
warehouse filled with "things" —stars, planets, black holes, and clouds of gas. These are the
artifacts on display. When we look at the empty space between them, we label it "nothing," or if
we detect mass we cannot see, we label it "dark."

This is a noun-based cosmology. It asks: What is it?

But this framing is a cognitive trap. The universe is not a collection of static objects; it is a
dynamic unfolding of processes. To understand the 95% of the universe that is currently invisible
to us, we must move from an ontology of substance to an ontology of action. This shift aligns
with a lineage of thought stretching from Heraclitus to Whitehead’s process philosophy: the
recognition that reality is not a stack of objects, but a flow of events.

To access this reality, we turn to the ancient Aristotelian distinction between the three modes of
activity:

1. Poiesis (Making): Action that transforms possibility into structure.
2. Praxis (Doing): Action that transforms structure into motion.
3. Theoria (Seeing): Action that transforms motion into meaning.

Modern cosmology has struggled because we are obsessed with the visible —the realm of
Theoria. But the visible universe (the 5% we can see) is merely the audience. The "Dark Sector"
(the 95%) is the actor.

The universe is not a museum of things; it is a choreography of Poiesis (The Architect) and
Praxis (The Breath), staged for the awakening of Theoria (The Witness).

Il. THE PROBLEM WITH “DARK” — A NAMING ERROR

Language is the operating system of thought. If you corrupt the file name, you corrupt the user’s
ability to access the file. In cosmology, we have committed a profound naming error by labeling
the dominant features of our reality Dark Matterand Dark Energy.

Historically, "Dark" was an admission of ignorance—a label for "that which does not interact
with light." But to the human mind, "Dark" implies a void, a negation, or a spooky cellar. It
suggests that the majority of reality is hidden from us.

This is a category error. Nouns describe statics; verbs describe systems. In a universe defined by
motion, a noun-based ontology is an inherent distortion. These forces are not "dark" because they
are hiding; they are "dark" because they are the machinery, not the exhibit. We do not see the
girders inside a skyscraper, nor the breath inside a lung, yet these are not "absences." They are
the fundamental requirements of presence.

We must stop asking what the darkness is, and start observing what the darkness does.
II. POIESIS: THE VERBS OF THE ARCHITECT (Dark Matter)

Poiesis is the act of making. It is the type of action that results in a structure distinct from the act
itself—like a carpenter building a house or a loom weaving cloth.

When we observe the gravitational behavior of "Dark Matter" through this lens, we see pure
Poiesis.

e It Scaffolds: In the early universe, it formed the original lattice —the cosmic web—that
allowed baryonic matter to pool and ignite into stars.

e It Binds: It acts as the gravitational glue, holding spinning galaxies together that would
otherwise fly apart.

° It Anchors: It creates the massive, invisible halos that cradle every galaxy.
In the Axis of Praxis, Dark Matter functions as The Architect.

The Architect is the Conservative Principle of the cosmos: the impulse to draw together, to
create form, to establish a center. It is not a "dark" substance that obscures; it is a structural force
that builds.

IV. PRAXIS: THE VERBS OF THE BREATH (Dark Energy)

Praxis is the act of doing. It is action where the activity itself is the goal—like dancing, living, or
breathing. It creates nothing but its own continuation.

When we observe the expansionist behavior of "Dark Energy," we see pure Praxis.
e It Stretches: It continuously expands the fabric of spacetime.

e It Dilutes: It introduces space between the great clusters, preventing the cosmos from
crunching down into a singular knot.

e — It Paces: It sets the tempo of the universe, driving the current era of acceleration.

In the Axis of Praxis, Dark Energy functions as The Breath.

The Breath is the Liberal Principle of the cosmos: the impulse to create space, to allow
distance, to prevent the structure from becoming a cage.

Together, Poiesis and Praxis form the Systole and Diastole of the universe —the necessary
tension of the Hold (Gravity) and the necessary release of the Open (Expansion).

V. THEORIA: THE VERBS OF THE WITNESS (Baryonic Matter)

If Dark Matter is the Stage-Builder (Poiesis) and Dark Energy is the Space-Maker (Praxis), what
is the 5% of the universe we can actually see? What are the stars, the planets, and us?

We are the agents of Theoria.

Theoria is not passive observation; it is the active labor of Witnessing. It is the universe
attempting to see itself. In philosophical terms —rather than biological or neurological ones — we
can view baryonic matter as the arena where sufficient complexity arises for the work of Theoria
to occur.

From a teleopoetic perspective, the system appears elegantly staged: The universe employs 95%
of its energy building the theatre (Poiesis) and widening the stage (Praxis), and under this
metaphor, the remaining 5% becomes the locus of Seeing(Theoria).

The "Dark Sector" provides the Conditions of Existence; the "Light Sector" provides the
Experience of Existence.

VI. THE AXIS OF PRAXIS — A DYNAMIC BALANCE

These forces do not exist in isolation. They form a single continuum of action, a sliding scale of
cosmic behavior:

POIESIS (Architect)—The Axis of Praxis—,PRAXIS (Breath)
(Hold / Structure) — (Release / Freedom)
Cosmic history is the movement of the slider along this axis.

° The Early Universe (Era of Poiesis): Gravity dominated. The scaffold was built. The
Architect was in charge.

¢ The Late Universe (Era of Praxis): The density dropped. The expansion accelerated.
The Breath took over.

Cosmology is not a tale of darkness. It is a dynamic balance between the necessity of the Hold
and the liberty of the Release.

VII. EMOTIONAL & COGNITIVE PAYOFF

Why does this reframing matter? Why replace accepted physics terms with philosophical verbs?

6 of 7

Because metaphors shape minds. When we teach "Dark Matter," we teach students that they
inhabit an unintelligible void. But when we teach the Cosmic Trinity, we reveal a universe that
rhymes with the human experience.

¢ — Intelligibility: We replace the fear of the unknown with an appreciation of function. Just
as we understand "gravity" not as a fluid but as the behavior of curvature, we can
understand the Dark Sector as the behavior of structure and expansion.

¢ Mirroring: We realize that Poiesis and Praxis are not alien forces; they are the same
impulses we feel in ourselves—the urge to build a life (Structure) and the urge to be free
(Expansion).

° Interface: This model is not a replacement for the Standard Model (ACDM); it is a
conceptual interface for it. It allows us to grasp the system without needing to solve the
particle physics first.

VII. TOWARD A VERB-BASED LEXICON

Names in science shift as models evolve; the "aether" gave way to "fields," and "corpuscles"
gave way to "photons." For the purpose of public understanding, philosophy, and education, we
offer a new lexicon that aligns name with action:

° Dark Matter — The Architect (Poiesis Field)
e — Dark Energy — The Breath (Praxis Field)
° Baryonic Matter — The Witness (Theoria Field)

Better names do not just describe the world; they invite future conceptual innovation by
removing the cognitive blocks of the past.

IX. CONCLUSION — PRACTICE MAKES A UNIVERSE

We have spent decades staring into the cosmos asking, "What is it?" We have searched for the
noun—the particle, the fluid, the stuff.

But the universe keeps moving. It keeps binding galaxies with invisible strings. It keeps
stretching spacetime with invisible breath. And through us, it keeps opening its eyes to watch.

The Axis of Praxis suggests that we stop treating the cosmos as a puzzle of hidden identity and
start treating it as a display of overt behavior. The Architect builds, the Breath expands, and the

Witness sees. These three verbs braid together to form the single chord of reality.

The darkness is not a void; it is a canvas for action. The matter is not missing; it is Making. The
energy is not mysterious; it is Doing. And we are not lost; we are Seeing.

The universe is not made of darkness. It is made of practice.
