ALEX university restarts this weekend

2023-01-07

Photo by Diego PH on Unsplash

To: ALEX U students

This weekend, ALEX university resumes after our holiday break!

Today (Saturday), Mark Brahmin and I discuss the Spielberg/Kubrick collaboration, AI. On Sunday, our Nietzsche course begins with Section II of Thus Spoke Zarathustra. Times are the same (3 PM ET), as is the Zoom logins, which are available on the ALEX website, to which you gained access after enrolling.

Onward!

RS
