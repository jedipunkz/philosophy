SLEEPING PIECE I

Write all the things you want
to do. Ask others to do them
and sleep until they finish
doing them. Sleep as long as
you can.

Yoko Ono, 1960 winter

BECOMING AWARE BY
ACCOUNTING FOR
YOUR TIME

PREPARATION:

Buy something which allows

activities: a book, camera,
computer, video camera etc.

EVENT:

Keep a written, drawn, spoken,
computerized, photographed,
etc., record of how you spend
your time each day. Maintain
the diary for as long as you
need. Notice changes in your
awareness.

Linda Montano, 1988

DEHABITUATE

PREPARATION:

Think of something you do

EVENT:

Photograph or videotape

MAP PIECE

Draw an imaginary map. Put
a goal mark on the map where
you want to go. Go walking on

meet. The map must be follow-

be dropped altogether.

Ask your friends to write maps.
Give your friends maps.
