Karl Marx is indivisible. That is as true of the unity of the

In the first chapter of the Manifesto they showed the decisive,

organizes itsclf in the class struggle, It develops politically and

leads to the fact that the civil war, which in capitalist society is

In this manner Marx and Engels, in the Manifesto, showed

Above all, however, they showed the working class it had the
