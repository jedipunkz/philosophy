264 沙棘 子 之 觀察 試驗

Observation Test of Sea-backthorn (太 popzge Linn)

Hu Min-Fuand Liu Sin-Yie
TARI COA

Abstract: One line of sea-backthorn had been introduced from both Russia and
Hungary country for observation test. “The results showed that the plant of
Russia line was 120cm high with 10 branches on an average after planting one
year; Hungary line was 20cm high with 3 branches after planting 6 months.
According to the foregoing data of investigation, we confirmed these plants could
groWw in Taiwan; however, the advanced research, such as numerous seeds
collection continuously from foreign production area and fertilizer application
management, should be enhanced for establishing anew agricultural industry in
Taiwan.
