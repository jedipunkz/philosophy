of negotiations. Moscow is
also stressing "aggressive
plans" of NATO, with particular

Moscow has taken the gen-

In Bonn, Soviet Ambassador

maneuver.

The agreement suggests that

Congo drawn up with the Belgians.
This has never been worked out

over the degree of centraliza-

appears that a new constitution

INDIAN SEIZURE OF PORTUGUESE ENCLAVES

The collapse of Portuguese
oesistance in Goa on 20 December
ended India's quick military
venture, which had capped 14
years of unsuccessful prodding
aimed at eliminating the last
Buropean enclaves in the sub-
continent. The decision to
resort to armed force--made by
Nehru only at the last minute--
was a reluctant reversal of long~
standing Indian policy. It fol-
lowed a series of minor inci-
aents near Goa which were ex-
aggerated in the context of
India's current election cam-
paign.but which led to a massive
Indian military buildup on the
vorder.

Despite Western pressures
to avoid violence, continued
Portuguese intransigence evi-
dently led New Delhi to conclude
that it could obtain its objec-
tive only by military action.
Xyishna Menon, fighting for his
political life and his Defense
Ministry portfolio, appears to

have played a key role in fanning
the crisis and influencing Nehru's

ultimate decision. The enclaves
now are under the control of an
Indian military governor, and
steps are under way to provide
for a quick turnover to civilian
authority.

Portugal's appeal to the
UN Security Council, its only
meaningful recourse, was frus-
trated by the USSR's veto of
a resolution calling on India
to cease fire and withdraw its
forces; Ceylon, the UAR, and

Liberia voted with the USSR.

The failure of the UN to support
Portugal has generated a bitter

withdrawing from the organization

from New York. Lisbon has appar-
ently concluded that further UN
action would not be beneficial.

creasing signs that the Goa
crisis has led Lisbon to “take
a new look" at its foreign policy.

ports indicating that the regime
feels that Lisbon's allies could
‘wave done more to deter Nehru,

Minister Nogueira informed
Elbrick on 15 December of the
"possibility of a fundamental
change in foreign policy";
