Abstract: The main purpose of this study was to investigate the effects of four kinds of deep
soll ploughing (20cm, 30cm, 40cm and CK) on growth and yield of two Aloe varieties
KKPH85-2(47oe roresycezzey) and KPHS83-1(4. cezszs)in Penghu. The results showed that
the yield components of Aloe variety were significantly different after Six months planting。
KPH85-2, which is imported from the U.S.A., had the higher yield components of plant
height leaf size and number than KPH83-1, but no significant differences on the effects 0f
different deep soil ploughing. Significant differences were found after one year; the best

Tesults of soil plough on the growth of Aloe were cultivation 30cm and 40cm deep.
