The following is the translation of instructions from the Metropolitan Committee
of the PCB to all units of the Party. in the Federal District, setting forth the
tasks to be carried out by the Party in preperation for a Convention for National
Emancipation, to be held in the Federal District. A National Convention

was planned for 15 January 195) in Rio.

TASKS FOR THE FEDERAL DISTRICT CONVENTION FOR NATIONAL EMANCIPATION,
A PART OF THE CAMPAIGN FOR THE RE-ESTABLISHMENT OF RELATIONS WITH
THE SOVIET UNION.

1. Each enterprise, district committee, and cell should organize danonstrations
in mass organizations: Feminine Unions, Commissions against the High Cost of
Living, Petroleum Commissions, Commissions of the Movement of Aid to the
Popular Press (MAIP), union commissions, Enterprise Commissions, and Enterprise
Syndical Councils. Demonstrations should be promoted by professiqnal commissions,
which we must create for the functioning of the Convention, made up of
journalists, lawyers, doctors, dentists, engineers, artists, profassors, and
students. We must promote round tables of businessmen and industrialists.

2. We must promote the launching of manifestos and appeals of adhesion to the
campaign, by enterprise, sector, or professional category, and by neighborhoods.
These manifestos and appeals should be edited by each organization. Commissions
should be created to raise the specific problems connected with the themes
under debate, which are resumption of relations with the Soviet Union and
national independence.
