NEAR EAST - AFRICA

Comment on coming Iraqi elections:

The Iraqi Acting Foreign Minister clearly stated
in a recent broadcast the readiness of his government to hold the promised
direct elections by 27 February 1953,-as required by law. This state-
ment invalidates previous intimations that recently appointed Prime
Minister Mahmoud would postpone the elections indefinitely.

Holding the elections, however, does not neces-
sarily mean that General Mahmoud, who was brought to power by the Regent
and elder statesman Nuri Said,is prepared to relinquish control of the gov-
ernment. He may: be confident that while the populace will derive some
satisfaction from direct elections, nevertheless, his control of the electoral
machinery will ensure his return to the premiership.
