## Abstract

The cyclical law of history is a cyclical phenomenon presented by the state power through the rise and fall of governance and chaos, and repeated cycles. How to jump out of the cyclical law of history is a major historical and practical subject that the Communist Party of China has been actively exploring over the past century. Marx wrote the deafening Communist Manifesto in 1848, and his theory of the mutual turnover of class struggles contains the root of the existence and continuity of the cyclical law of history, and has had a profound impact on the Marxists of later generations. Taking the important assertions in the Communist Manifesto as the logical starting point for thinking, combining them with the relevant theories of classic Marxist writers and Chinese Marxism, and then exploring the logical necessity for the CPC to break out of the cyclical law of history, it is of great practical significance for a better understanding of the CPC's guidelines and policies as well as scientific governance in the new era.
