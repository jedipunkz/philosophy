GERMANY Must PerisH presents a plan
for the structure of a permanent and lasting
peace among civilized nations. It bases its thesis
on the eventual defeat of Germany by the British
Empire and its Allies, without the assistance
of the United States.

However should circumstances decree that
the American public cast its ballot in favor of war
as a measure of self-defense (and it is the fer-

Slogan but a Solemn Purpose and a Sacred
Promise.

Let that Purpose be an Enduring Peace!
