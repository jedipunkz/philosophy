I. Paris Commune

A pure insurrection, and I mean a pure event. But I also mean the fused
group and the pure model of its government of the proletariat and its
attacking stance with pikes and barricades and councils and long history
then became a failure.

After that I prefer a normal simple Communist party which does no work
for the proletariat but economic reforms and piecemeal reforms.

II. But then what is the Commune can become a Normal Model

I mean this is my essential point - Commune as daily politics but
somehow a normal protest model party.
