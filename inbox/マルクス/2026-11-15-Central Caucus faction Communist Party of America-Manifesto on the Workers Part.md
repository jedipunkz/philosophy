MANIFESTO

of the Communist Party of America
on The Workers' Party

TO THE WORKERS OF AMERICA:

COMRADES AND FELLOW-WORKERS:—

The history of the labor movement in America is the history of the
betrayal of the interests of the working class by self-appointed "leaders".
The revolutionary vanguard of the workers has been cursed with reformism
and opportunism. Many political organizations and parties have sprung
up, all claiming to represent the working class, and appealing for support
on the basis of programmes which represented "short cuts" to the emanci-
pation of the workers from the horrors of wage slavery. During the
period preceding the world war all these Utopian schemes and blue prints
of the "Co-operative Commonwealth" and the "Workers' Republic" and
short cuts to the revolution could win some measure of support from cer-
tain elements of the workers and the petit-bourgeoisie because none of
these theories had been subjected to any test of action. With the success-
ful proletarian revolution in Russia in 1917 and the establishment of the
first proletarian government of Soviet Russia all the fine spun theories of
the; social-patriots, reformers and Utopians were exploded. The prole-
tarian revolution in Russia is the beacon which must guide the working
class of all countries in the employment of the correct tactics for the
speedy and successful overthrow of the Capitalist State, and the living
example of the form and character of the government to be established
by the working class. This workers' government can be no other than the
dictatorship of the proletariat in the form of Soviet Power.

In 1919 the Communist International was organized and there took
place throughout the world a realignment of the revolutionary forces
formerly represented by the socialist and social-democratic parties in
every country. In the light of the experience of the Russian workers and
peasants the new strategy for revolutionary effort was demonstrated and
the principle of Marxian Socialism firmly established. In response to the
clarion call of the Communist International, the "left wing" of the Socialist
Parties formed themselves into Communist Parties in each country as
sections of the Communist International, thus forming one international
Communist Party of disciplined and determined revolutionists, with a prac-
tical program of action, both immediate and ultimate in scope.

In the United States, the Communist Party of America was organized
in Chicago, September, 1919, composed of elements which accepted the
principles of the Communist International and which had left the reformist

difference in methods and in aims. The Communists clearly and fearlessly

only achieve their emancipation from all the horrors of capitalism through
mass action which will develop into open armed conflict of the workers
against the forces of the capitalist state, resulting in its violent overthrow
and destruction, establishing in its place the dictatorship of the proletariat

The program of the Workers' Party contains nothing but a mere
Platonic aspiration to have a "Workers' Republic" in America. Although
this spurious party is only a few months old, its official organs admit that
their program is negative in character and does not point the way to the
emancipation of the proletariat and the abolition of capitalism. To make
up for this the Workers' Party sets up the claim that they have a set of
"fighting leaders" who personally will guarantee the integrity and revolu-
tionary character of the Workers' Party. The curse of the American pro-
letariat has always been their dependence upon individuals who have from
time to time set themselves up as the saviours and self-appointed leaders
of the workers. The Workers' Party stands self-confessed as a fraud and
an imposition. The Workers' Party gives lip service to the Soviet Govern-
ment of Russia and to the Communist International, while at the same
time repudiating everything for which the Russian Revolution and the
Communist International stands.

The Communist Party of America, Section of the Communist Inter-
national categorically declares that the Workers' Party cannot and does
not speak for, or in any way represent the revolutionary proletariat of
America, that it does not support or advocate the principles and tactics of
the Communist International ; that the Workers' Party is not composed of
revolutionary proletarian elements, but that instead it is a centrist sect
composed of the personal following of a few semi-bourgeois intellectuals
who have nff sfc&hding in the labor movement of America.

The CoiSmunist Party of America, therefore, repudiates the Workers'
Party and calls upon the class conscious workers of America to have noth-
ing to do with this organization. This so-called Workers' Party claims to
support the Red Trade Union International and is attempting to work
within the trade unions and other organizations of the workers in this
country. The Red Trade Union International is built upon the principle
of the Proletarian Dictatorship based upon Soviet Power which the Work-
ers' Party repudiates as a tactic for the proletariat of America, and con-
sequently the Communist Party of America warns the workers organized
in the trade unions against the activities of the Workers' Party which
cannot and does not speak in the name of the R, T. U. I.

The Communist Party of America, Section of the Communist Inter-
national, is composed of working men and women, who are conscious and
determined revolutionists. The Communist Party is an underground party,
declared illegal by the Capitalist Government of the U. S., it refuses to
emasculate its program in order to conform to the requirements of bour-
geois legality. In spite of the underground, illegal character of the Com-
munist Party it has organized the necessary legal apparatus with which
to carry its message to the workers and to participate in the day to day
struggle against our exploiters. The members of the Communist Party
are working side by side with their fellow workers in shop, mill, mine,
factory, field and railroad. They are active in the everyday struggles of
their fellows, fighting shoulder to shoulder with them in the trade and
industrial unions against the reactionary and traitorous leaders, in strikes,
in demonstrations, in every working class activity.

The Communist Party has no intention of attempting to raise up
"new saviours" or imposing a few intellectuals upon the workers or their
organizations as "fighting leaders". The Communist Party has no inter-
ests separate or apart from those of the broad masses of the exploited
workers. We are bone of their bone and flesh of their flesh. Any influence
or leadership achieved by the Communists will be won by the proven de-
votion, self-sacrifice and loyalty to the cause of the workers.

The Communist Party calls upon all workers who accept its revolu-
tionary principles and its tactics to join its ranks. The Communist Party
recognizes that there is no easy road to Communism. The struggle of the
exploited masses is a life and death struggle with the capitalist class and
their capitalist government. In this struggle the workers must present a
united front against their enemy. It is the aim of the Communist Party
to unite the exploited masses of America in their fight for emancipation
from capitalism. Any party or organization which seeks to break this
working class unity by spreading confusion in their ranks is counter-
revolutionary and an enemy of the proletariat.

The Proletarian Revolution in Russia has clarified and demonstrated
the tactics and the. principles upon which the world revolution will be
achieved. The Communist Party of America is committed by its program
and its activities to these principles as enunciated by the general staff of
the revolutionary world proletariat — the Communist International. The
Communist Party will oppose all parties and organizations which oppose
these principles and will expose those dangerous conf usionists and centrists
who pretend to support the Communist International in order to increase
their personal prestige with the workers, only to emerge from their hiding
places at an opportune time to stab the Communist Party and the labor
movement in the back.

The so-called Workers' Party is a menace to the proletariat of America.
It is a party of dangerous compromisers, opportunists and centrists, mask-
ing themselves as Communists. The Communist Party of America calls
upon all workers and workers' organizations to refuse to support or to join
this prototype of the decadent Socialist Party — this aggregation of Com- •
promisers, opportunists and centrists — this party of the American Men-
sheviki called the Workers' Party of America.

Down with the betrayers of the proletarian revolution.

Rally to the revolutionary banners of the Communist Party of America.

Long live the Communist International.

Issued by the CENTRAL EXECUTIVE COMMITTEE
of the COMMUNIST PARTY OF AMERICA,

DAVIS, Executive Secretary.
