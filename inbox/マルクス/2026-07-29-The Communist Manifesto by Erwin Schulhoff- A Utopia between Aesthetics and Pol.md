## Abstract

The article is devoted to the oratorio “The Communist Manifesto” op. 82 (1932) by the Czech composer Erwin Schulhoff. Its Russian premiere (Nizhny Novgorod, 2024) gives reason to turn to this undeservedly forgotten work, which combines many key trends in the art of the 1930s, primarily political engagement and a new interpretation of the synthesis of arts, put at the service of music for the masses.
