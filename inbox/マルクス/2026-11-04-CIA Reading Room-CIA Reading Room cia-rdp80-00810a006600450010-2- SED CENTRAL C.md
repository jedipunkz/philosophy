1. The Department of Propaganda and Sciences (Abteilung Propaganda und
Wissenschaften) of the SED Central Committee is located on the fifth

floor of the Zentral-Haus der Einheit, Berlin N 5), Wilhelm-Pieckstrasse 1.
Professor Dr. Kurt Hager is head of this department. He is responsible

for the guidance and direction of the East German State Secretariat for 25X1
Advanced Schools (Staatssekretariat fuer Hochsshulwesen).

2- Helmut Fromiknecht~ dis the employee of this department responsible
for matters dealing with SED-sanctioned periodicals published by the
Volk und Wissen publishing house. Frommknecht is very polite and
cooperative but seems to know little about the more specialised aspects
of the publishing business.

25X11

Comments Fromuknecht first worked in a large plant in
gsdorf as a fulletime SED Party Secretary after his return from

the USSR, where he had graduated from an Antifa school, In 1949 he

joined the Propaganda Department of the SED Land Directorate Brandene

burg and in October 1950 he was delegated to attend the two-year course

at the Karl Marx Party Academy in Klein-Machnow. Upon completion of the

course he was assigned to the cadre section of the Propaganda Department

of the SED Central Committee, where until mid-1953 he was responsible

for the selection and placement of students of the Party Academy and of

the Institute for Social Sciences. 25X11.
