Preface

When, after the ill-fated World War, a rabble of convicts gathered by the Jew had seized
power, when cowardice and meanness triumphed, while courage and loyalty were despised,
it was significant that the cowed bourgeoisie meekly submitted to subhumanity. A few
courageous men had the courage to raise their voices against the consummate opprobrium
and, relying exclusively on themselves, declared war on the criminality that instigated
subversion. One of these men was Julius Streicher. As early as November 1918, he gathered
former officers around him and awakened new faith and new life in the desperate. In 1919,
he participated as a speaker in the debates of the Communist-Bolshevik assemblies in
Nuremberg. In 1920, he visited many cities in the Reich and preached about German
socialism. He made all these countless sacrifices in addition to his professional work and
with his own resources. Wherever he went in the German countryside, everywhere, people
awoke as if from a nightmare.

They began to think again, to hope again, and to believe again. I have collected a number
of these speeches from the period of struggle, which often lasted well over an hour and were
delivered with immense enthusiasm and captivating power, in this book. Today's reader will
be filled with admiration when they see how Julius Streicher prophetically foresaw political
events twenty years ago.

They will be amazed at the fierce constancy with which he spoke, whether in Leipzig in
1920, in Munich on 9 November 1923, in the Bavarian parliament in 1927, or in the
congress hall in Nuremberg in 1932. But no one can imagine that many of these speeches
were delivered at public rallies in the midst of a proletarian mob, whipped up and brutalised
by the Jews, and in the provincial parliament before a depraved bourgeoisie, a clergy that
laughed scornfully, and a red bonzism (that had grown fat through laziness!

What it meant then to express one's opinion so frankly: this cannot be described in words,
it is carried only in one's heart, that man who even today hears the echo of the corrosive
sneer, the howl of anger and the threats of his adversaries. All honest seekers, who at that
time could not yet understand Streicher, will understand today, when the Jew incites the
peoples to a new feast of murder, that they had been blinded. They will recognise today that
the man they had mocked and refused to believe was right when, for two decades, he
repeatedly and unceasingly designated the Jew as the enemy of the world. May this book
find wide resonance and become for young people, especially for future generations, the
legacy of a man who, in the most difficult times, with ardent love and enthusiasm in his
heart, showed our German people the way to the future!

On the Ist Reich Party Day, in the Reich of Greater Germany, Nuremberg, September
1938.

Dr Heinz Preiss

The greatest danger to a movement are
those who instil fear and pessimism.

Speech delivered on 1 August 1920, in Leipzig

(Julius Streicher began in Nuremberg in November 1918 as a speaker at independent
meetings and those of the majority socialists, engaging in political struggle. After he had
spoken successfully several times, Jewish officials recognised him as a dangerous opponent
and no longer allowed him to speak. However, in order to continue expressing his opinion
publicly, Streicher had to join a party.

In the spring of 1920, members of the German Socialist Party invited him to join their
ranks. Before Streicher joined, the German Socialist Party (*) was, like the German
Workers’ Party before Adolf Hitler joined, a bourgeois national association without
fighting spirit or enthusiasm. Just as Adolf Hitler did in the German Workers' Party, Julius
Streicher transformed the German Socialist Party into a suitable fighting community. On
his initiative, the German Socialist Party groups scattered throughout the Reich united into
one association. On 4 June 1920, he founded his first militant newspaper, the Deutscher
Sozialist. Streicher had to assert himself not only against political opponents, but also
against numerous ignorant and lukewarm members within his own party. For them in
particular, the inflexibility with which Julius Streicher wanted to wage the struggle against
Judaism was incomprehensible and dangerous for the party's survival.

From 31 July to 2 August 1920, the 2nd Party Day took place in Leipzig. At this meeting,
Streicher gave a fundamental speech on his position regarding Judaism. But this speech
also reveals the difficulties Julius Streicher had to face at the beginning of his struggle.

My fellow Germans! I greet you on behalf of the combative newspaper Deutscher
Sozialist. At the 1st Party Day in Hanover on 25 April 1920, the decision was made to
found a daily newspaper. It was to bring the members of our movement closer together, win
new supporters and repel the attacks of our opponents. We then deliberated on when the
newspaper should appear and what its name should be. In the meantime, I have been able to
bring it to life. This is how the militant newspaper Deutscher Sozialist came into being.
Immense difficulties had to be overcome. There was a lack of money, materials and
experience. A lot of time-consuming correspondence was necessary to obtain paper. But all
these difficulties have been overcome. Although, of course, many new concerns have been
added. But the Deutscher Sozialist goes deep and fights for our idea.

I have therefore considered whether the combative newspaper would be a party
newspaper or an independent newspaper. I have decided on an independent newspaper. The
newspaper, therefore, does not belong to the party, but to the publisher. But if I were to be
put before a firing squad, the party would be the heir to the Deutscher Sozialist. 1 have taken
full responsibility for the paper. That is why I also demand that you, my fellow Germans,
place your trust in me! The question of the newspaper is a question of trust. You cannot
imagine how much the preparations have cost. But I had no money, and only with money
could the combative paper be kept alive. Who can therefore take offence if I have accepted
money from friends? We do not attack capital itself, of course: capital is certainly necessary
for everything. This matter is also a question of trust.

When I have been asked how I obtained the funds for the newspaper, I have always
answered openly that I obtained them after persistent requests. | am glad that the people
understand that our work is based on honesty and good intentions.

I know that the structure of our combative newspaper must be in accordance with the
party programme. However, I must demand complete freedom. As long as I am in charge of
the newspaper, I will only accept what I can take responsibility for. Nevertheless, I will
compose the newspaper in accordance with the party programme. I have received several
letters from readers advising me to avoid the word 'Jew' in future. I declare here that I have
always chosen the topics of my articles with careful consideration and have thought
carefully about every word. I have proceeded deliberately and I am well aware that I have a
difficult struggle ahead of me, but I do not fear it in any way. Gentlemen, consider that we
are dealing with an unenlightened working class and a so-called educated middle class! But
do not forget that the so-called educated middle class is an unenlightened middle class! In
other letters, readers demanded longer and more scientific articles. Gentlemen, but we want
to reach the worker first and foremost! Brevity is the spice of life for the worker. The
worker does not read long reports. Let us not emphasise what separates us, but write about
what unites us! I have deliberately kept the articles short and I urge you to proceed in this
manner. The content must be written in a popular style, otherwise it will not be understood
by the circles we want to attract. The name Helfferich was recently mentioned in the
Deutscher Sozialist. | did this deliberately. It was not a mistake. In general, anything that
could endanger the movement is kept out of the paper. But under no circumstances should
our paper lose its combative character!

Lately, Deutscher Sozialist has appeared irregularly. This was due to frequent changes in
the printing press. In addition, the supply of paper is extremely difficult. Despite this, I have
always managed to print and distribute the newspaper in very high circulation. I have
received enthusiastic support from all sides. | am glad that non-members also belong to our
circle of readers. Although the content may not be entirely satisfactory here and there, every
seeker will nevertheless learn something new. For I strive to make the content as
multifaceted as possible. Already, the brutal struggle of Judaism against our combative
paper is beginning. Newspaper sellers are being forced by the Jews not to sell our paper
anymore. It may be of interest to you to know that a minister already has the paper delivered
to him regularly. You see, therefore, how circles that do not belong to us are already turning
their attention to us! That is what I wanted. In order to keep the paper alive, I must appeal to
your spirit of sacrifice and ask you for voluntary contributions.

When many hands give something, a lot is collected. Raising money is immensely
important in view of the daily increasing cost of living. I recently went to see a
manufacturer and said to him, "Give me money for so many workers and I will send them
the newspaper!" The manufacturer agreed to this and only asked that his name not be
mentioned. This, of course, can be done without any problem. Try to do the same in your
circle of acquaintances and do not leave me alone in my heavy work full of responsibility!
Although we cannot do without money for the struggle, there is an even greater force for us.
This is the spirit that is inherent in us and that always drives us back into the struggle. The
people must feel that we are honest fighters. The greatest danger to a movement,

Without a solution to the racial question, there can be no true socialism.

Speech delivered on 13 November 1920, in the
Kurhaus hall, Bernburg

(As early as 1920, Julius Streicher spoke in many German cities about the national idea.
On 13 November 1920, he arrived in the mining town of Bernburg in Anhalt. Here, the
small German socialist group had called a meeting. Among those present, 80% were
opponents. After the chair of the meeting had said a few words of welcome, the Marxists
demanded that the chair be handed over to a majority socialist. Their intention was to
have the speaker's microphone switched off again after a few short statements. To prevent
the meeting from being broken up prematurely, the chair of the assembly had no choice
but to agree. As a result, a majority socialist took his place at the speaker's podium as
chair. He gave the floor to Julius Streicher.

Streicher spoke so powerfully and convincingly that the president did not even have a
chance to interrupt him. In this way, the Bernburg assembly became a memorable event of
that era, an event that revealed the full extent of the enthusiasm and determination of that
man who was still fighting alone.

Fellow German men and women! It is unusual for a legally constituted presidency to give
way to one imposed by force at a meeting convened in accordance with the rules. It is also
unusual for the speaker at a duly convened assembly to deliver his speech under the
chairmanship of a political opponent. But it makes no difference to me whether the chair is
occupied by majority socialists, independents or communists; all that matters is that they are
German men!

I am fully aware of the honour of being able to speak under the chairmanship of another
party as a German socialist!

At the beginning of my speech, I would like to mention how my opponents worked to
thwart this demonstration. A certain Bardowski, the editor-in-chief of the Marxist
newspaper Bernburger Volkswacht ‘°-wrote about our combative newspaper Deutscher
Sozialist that the name of the editor-in-chief — by which he meant me — was as stupid as the
content of his newspaper! Furthermore, this inflammatory paper called on the population to
remove me from the speaker's platform and whip me out of the hall. Such mockery of the
workers by the party of clowns could not be tolerated!

Mr Bardowski: I am not responsible for the fact that my father gave me his name and not
the much more beautiful name of Bardowski. But I value the workers of Bernburg more
highly than the Bernburger Volkswacht does. I believe that the serious men who descend
day after day into the eternal darkness of the potash mines do not whip a German socialist
out of the room because he has something to say that may not be welcome to the
Bernburger Volkswacht. But, German men and women: if, at the end, you feel that my
remarks are a mockery of the working class, then remove me from the podium and whip me
out of the hall!

If we want to understand the current subjugation of the working people, we must examine
the evolution over centuries in Germany. Mass poverty among workers can only be said to
have existed since the children of farmers who migrated to the industrial zone

had to earn their bread in the factories of the emerging big cities. Usury in land and the
exploitative spirit of money turned the worker and the employee into mercenaries. At that
time, strangers in terms of blood took charge of the German worker. Those of the same
blood, however, turned away from them. The sin of the bourgeoisie of that time still cries
out to heaven in blood red! It was a horrible crime! With the year of victory in 1871, a
development began in Germany that could not come to a good end in such an unhealthy and
associated popular life.

The last Hohenzollern was no longer a German Kaiser, but a German Sun King. He
tolerated Jewish international financiers in his entourage and thus became increasingly
alienated from his people. This unnatural development was bound to be followed by
collapse. This was evident in the unfortunate outcome of the World War and the subsequent
non-German revolution. When today we find ourselves disgraced and stand — people and
state — before a pile of rubble such as the world has only seen in times of extremely difficult
fate, it is because we have not paid attention to the racial laws instituted by God.
Knowledge of these facts, however, should not lead us to despair of our destiny. Together,
we must take courage to become doctors to our unfortunate, sick people and show them the
right path to a happy resurrection. The movement towards German socialism shows us the
way to this renewal of the state and the people.

German socialism puts an end to the fraud of Marxism's apparent socialism and pursues
social collaboration among all classes and professions. German socialists do not wage class
struggle, but rather race struggle, which we will wage on the spiritual plane. We will
prevent a racially inferior minority from dominating and exploiting our people! Without the
solution of the racial question, there is no true socialism and no true community life!

We do not fight capital itself, but only the abuse of money, as manifested in mammonism.
©) We will break down usurious capitalism, which through the effortless collection of
interest leads to the enslavement of entire peoples. Marxist socialism deliberately sidesteps
the solution to this issue and directs the anger of the incited masses towards value-
producing entrepreneurial money, pushing it all the more surely into the arms of the
octopuses of loan capitalism that surround the world. The nationalisation of finance will
at last! give the productive people their rights. Without free land, no people who love their
soil and their homeland can survive. Marxism needs an uprooted working class. Their
discontent is stirred up by global capitalism with satanic planning against everything that is
in accordance with order, and with this the Jew can remain in the stirrups. The entire people
are allowed to go hungry to serve the same purpose. A people that is undermined and
exhausted by hunger is completely prey to the Jew.

That is why we, the German socialists, demand the security of our food supplies! Food
supplies create labour power! Labour power creates food supplies! Those who deny the
people or practise usury with the necessary foodstuffs are guilty of murder.

The capable must be given free rein in the people. They must be given the opportunity to
become independent.

Through a gradual dismantling towards an economy of necessary goods, over-
industrialisation must be curbed.

Factories must be moved outside, to the countryside. Workers need light and air!

cunning. If we had a law based on the German popular consciousness again, the problem of

Finally, I would like to give you our slogan: it is called struggle! It applies to the

Anyone who, in this desperate situation, thinks only of themselves and forgets the welfare

My fellow countrymen! Even though we find ourselves in a situation that could not be

we fight and believe.

Our German people still have a great mission! The West has been declared dead. But it
must not die! The German people, once they have been purified and enlightened, will bring
salvation to the world and crush the head of the Midgard serpent!

The Jew is a stranger

Speech delivered on 23 November 1922, at the
Kolosseum in Nuremberg

(On 20 October 1922, Julius Streicher subordinated his movement, called the
German Labour Community, °) and his combative newspaper Deutscher Volkswille
©) to Adolf Hitler. Adolf Hitler's movement doubled in size through the selfless

the currency fell. Inflation and unemployment loomed like deadly shadows over the German
people. Thousands flocked to Julius Streicher's meetings in search of help, desperate to find
strength and vigour. Only one of these fiery, combative speeches from the early days of the
movement has been preserved. The criminal commissioner, Hans Hols, from Nuremberg,
wrote it down in the course of his duties. Hans Héls wrote the following police report about
the meeting on 23 November 1922: "Posters stuck up in public places announced a meeting

and profit. What a contrast between the German worker and the Jew! I always ask myself:
Why is the Jew not a worker? Why is he not part of the community of honest producers?
Why does he acquire wealth without

My fellow countrymen! A people born to haggle has no right to live among a people that

The Jews never took part in the destiny of our people. During the World War, the

But how are we to proceed against the Jewish exploiters? Our adversaries are spreading

Wilhelm II was surrounded by the Jews Ballin and Rathenau. They exerted the greatest
