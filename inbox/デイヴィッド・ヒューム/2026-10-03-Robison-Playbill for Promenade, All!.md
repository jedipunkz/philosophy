from the nearby seacoast too, that seemed so distant then. Oh, and the music. What music there was and it seemed to set the
pace for life and float like the very balloon held so tightly in your hand. And life was special then. Remember? If you remember,

HUME CRONYN

Born in London, Ontario and educated at
Ridley College and McGill University, Hume
Cronyn is a man of many talents andhe has
had ready recognition of his gifts. A star of
stage and screen, he has also enjoyed suc-
cess as a director, writer and producer.
During his three decades-plus in all divi-
sions of the performing arts, he has ap-
peared or starred in twenty-eight Broad-
way productions, a score of films, and has
directed a dozen plays.

Mr. Cronyn’s first adventure in acting
was as an undergraduate member of The
Player’s Club of McGill University. A role
in a Washington stock company’s produc=
tion of UP POPS THE DEVIL made him a
professional. After completing his training
at the American Academy of Dramatic Arts,
he met his first Broadway audience in the
role of the janitor in HIPPER’S HOLIDAY.
Thirty years later in 1964, he received the
Academy’s Ninth Annual Award for Achieve-
ment by an alumnus. One of his most recent
Broadway appearances was in Edward
Albee’s Pulitzer Price winner, A DELI-
CATE BALANCE, in which he co-starred
with Jessica Tandy (Mrs. Cronyn). Another
memorable co-starring association was
THE FOURPOSTER, which repeated its
long-run success on tour and grew up into
a musical I DO, I DO. Among Mr. Cronyn’s
recent roles were Jimmy Luton in BIG
FISH, LITTLE FISH; Newton in THE PHY-
SICISTS, and Polonius in HAMLET with
Richard Burton for which he won that year’s
Tony Award and also the Variety Poll of
Critics vote. His classical roles include
HAMLET on an ANTA Tour, RICHARD III,
Harpagon in THE MISER, Tchebutykin in
THE THREE SISTERS and Yepihodov in
THE CHERRY ORCHARD for the Tyrone
Guthrie Theatre in Minneapolis, and Pro-
zorrov in THE THREE SISTERS in New
York, He has also been well-favoured with
feature roles in many other outstanding
American plays including THREE MEN ON
A HORSE, BOY MEETS GIRL, HIGH TOR,
GOLDEN BOY, THE MALE ANIMAL, MAD-=

AME WILL YOU WALK and as Willy Loman
in DEATH OF A SALESMAN. His director-
ial credits include NOW I LAY ME DOWN
TO SLEEP, which starred Frederic March
and Florence Eldridge; THE EGGHEAD,
TRIPLE PLAY, HILDA CRANE, MADAME
WILL YOU WALK and PORTRAIT OF A
MADONNA.

Invited to Hollywood by Alfred Hitchcock
for a role in SHADOW OF DOUBT, Mr.
Cronyn was kept busy there for several
years and his third film THE SEVENTH
CROSS brought him a nomination for an
Oscar. He was prominent in LIFEBOAT,
THE POSTMAN RINGS TWICE, BRUTE
FORCE, SUNRISE AT CAMPOBELLO, THE
GREEN YEARS and a variety of comedies.
During that period he also contributed
screen treatments for ROPE and UNDER
CAPRICORN. His most recent motion pic-
tures are THE ARRANGEMENT, GAILY,
GAILY and THERE WAS A CROOKED MAN.

Mr. Cronyn appeared on television as early
as 1939 as Ned Farrar in HER MAJESTY’S
VOICE on NBC. He repeated his FOUR-
POSTER role on television andhis Polonius
as well. He has appeared indramatic works
on all the major networks including seven
appearances on OMNIBUS, THE BRIDGE OF
SAN LUIS REY and he co-starred with Sir
Laurence Olivier in THE MOON AND SIX-
PENCE.

Mr. Cronyn appeared as Fr. William
Rolfe in HADRIAN VII, with The Stratford
National Theatre Co. of Canada in Straford,
Aug. 1969 and National Tour 1969-1970. He
played Capt. Queeg in CAINE MUTINY
COURT MARTIAL at the Ahmanson Thea-
tre, Los Angeles 1971 and in 1972, first
created his roles in PROMENADE ALL at
the Alvin Theatre in New York City.

JESSICA TANDY

Jessica Tandy got her theatrical start in
England touring through the provinces and
made her first professional appearance in
London. Subsequently she went to New York
and for the next several years divided her
time between performances in America and
England, playing many Shakespearan hero-
ines as well as roles in contemporary dra-
mas. She created the role of Blanche Dubois
in ASTREETCAR NAMED DESIRE for which
she won the Tony award.
