PREFACE

This report summarizes significant items of photo intelligence
relating to the North Vietnamese railroad system which has been
developed from available photography (received by the CIA Imagery
Analysis Division) during the period indicated on the cover. This
report is intended to provide a comprehensive view of the railroad
system and does not necessarily reflect the most recent status
changes as reported by pilots' reports or field photo intelligence
cables.

The railroad system has been broken down into 5 major lines:
the Hanoi/Lao Cai Line, the Hanoi/Dong Dang Line, the Hanoi/
Haiphong Line, the Hanoi/Dong Hoi Line, and the Thai Nguyen
Line (Figure !). Annotated maps and photos are used to show the
Status of each line.

SUMMARY

One bridge interdiction was observed on the Hanoi/Dong Hoi Rail
Line. Those portions of the Hanoi/Lao Cai and Hanoi/Dong Dang Rail Lines
covered by interpretable photography were open to through traffic. Probable
Standard gauge associated construction activity is underway in the vicinity
of Yen Vien on the Hanoi/Dong Dang Rail Line.

The Yen Bay Railroad Yard has been heavily damaged
subsequent to 7 November 1966 (Report No. 21). The previously reported
damage has been repaired and the damaged and destroyed rolling stock has
been removed. Current damage includes the cratering of a large portion
of the yard and interrupted through service; however, the choke points
are intact. Two probable locomotives and 2 gondola cars have been damaged,
and at least 3 gondola cras have been destroyed. Seven gondola cars, 1 box
car, and 1 probable flat car are undamaged.
