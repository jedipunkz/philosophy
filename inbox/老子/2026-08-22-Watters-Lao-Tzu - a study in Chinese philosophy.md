One of the most remarkable men in the history of China,
as also in the history of philosophy, is Lao-tzii, the author of

an incarnation of deity, showing himself on this earth at
sundry times and in various manners. His conception and
birth, his personal appearance, and everything about him

philosophic little treatise which he composed is regarded as
a sacred book. Much of this has arisen from a spirit of
rivalry with Buddhism. The Taoists did not wish to be be-
