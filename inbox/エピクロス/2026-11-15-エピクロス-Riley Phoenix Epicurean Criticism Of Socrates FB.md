Now the logical consequences of this refusal to attribute reality to the

the clues offered by sight, whereas to be consistent he should ignore this
illusion and follow what is really real, whatever the consequences. Thus
the basic fault of Socrates is not wrong belief but the inconsistency
between his belief and his practice.
