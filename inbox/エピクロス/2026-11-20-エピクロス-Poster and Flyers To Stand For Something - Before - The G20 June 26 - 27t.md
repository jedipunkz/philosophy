Thanks to trillions in taxpayer-funded bankster bailouts
during this recession, most people know who runs the
world. Now: we're trying to figure out what to do about
their bosses. Mainstream stories like "Bilderberg 2010:
Between the sword and the wall: The Catalan police
are refreshingly friendly. But if the time for action
comes, whose side will they be on?" in the London
Guardian (5/Jun/10) reveal the worlds rulers just met in
Sitges, Spain, like they have yearly since 1954 (BBC)
to discuss "globalization", or centralizing their power.
The Canadian media covered their meeting near
Ottawa in 2006. See: the documentary film "Endgame".

Millions worldwide have, so anybody can just look into
these issues. If they think the info is stupid, they can
laugh at it. If they think it's smart, they can appreciate
it. A Spanish police officer couldn't stand guarding the
Bilderberg meeting and quit. Other cops said they
didn't like them and were just doing their job. Our G20
"world leaders" are their "salesmen" used to convince
us to "compete" in the "new world order". They could've
held the G20 June 26 - 27th in a remote area to save
Toronto the trouble. The $1 billion in "security" and big
"protest" are just a traumatic slap in the face to the city.
So... what should Canadians stand for before the G20?

In 1954, the U.S. Congress Carroll Reece Commission
revealed that wealthy Americans only created the Ford,
Rockefeller and other "foundations" to use "grants" to
control "activism" and "academia" and let our problems
get worse. It's the same worldwide. We can update the
old "protest" or "rock concert" model that teaches well-
informed people to get together, yell and go home. We
can just calmly and consistently share our info instead.

In 2010, all Canadians are threatened by health risks,
the recession and more. Since many people think this
is heavy stuff: should Canadian men age 25 - 45 deal
with it on behalf of women, children and the elderly? ©

Anybody can try what's been working so far, or put up
posters, hand out flyers and dvd's, take pictures, make
videos, communicate online. We can share info to
improve our health, wealth and relationships. We can
use "inspiration" for more than just "entertainment". We
don't have to discuss any of this if we don't want to. We
can just pass on good info so that our fellow Canadians
have the chance to see it. Calmly and consistently... ©
