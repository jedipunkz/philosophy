Did you ever stop to wonder who sent out all the cards
and set the Jay and arranged the program for the family
reunions which ‘flourish with unequalled vigor in this part of
the country? Perhaps you thought those delightful events—
delightful if it didn't rain or wasn’t too hot, or a family skeleton
wasn't pulléd out of the closet, just happened. You're all
wrong then. Meet George L. Randall, who speaks with author-
ity -on family reunions, since he not only arranges most of
those held in these parts, but sven attends them and still lives
to tell the story.

«Mr. Randall didn’t start arranging reunions or his other
work: of. solving genealogical puzzles until eighteen years
| ago. But he- had an aunt and the aunt had a passionate
desire to possess one of those family trees which have big
branches and little branches and twigs and tendrils, and then
sometimes aren’t big enough to hold every member of the
family. That was the way it turned out to be with the Ran-
dails. When that tree started growing, after Mr. Randall had
explored the roots and loosened the’ soil around it, it didn’t
feem to know where to stop. It’s still climbing and his aunt
must have Had to give up all hope of confining within the
slender compass of those framed specimens of genealogical trees
all the ramifications of the Randall family.

Naturally when you have such a lot of relatives to look up

- you stumble over many other families and sometimes collect
) interesting data about them. This is what happened to Mr.
y Randall, who has traveled as far west as St. Louis, and has

scoured all New England and New York state in pursuit of
his elusive forebears.
A Use for Reunions.

He decided to go into the business as it were, and thought
the best way to go about it was to begin by arranging reunions,
and getting whole families together to talk things over. It is

: no simple matter to arrange one of these huge affairs. He has
Py 1200 members of one family to notify and the rest of them
4 are close to the thousand mark. All sorts of helpful bits of
