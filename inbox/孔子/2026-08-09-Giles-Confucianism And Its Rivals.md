In the following Lectures an attempt is made to
exhibit, chronologically, the principles and practice
adopted by Confucius' as a heritage from antiquity
and subsequently handed down through twenty-
four centuries, with certain modifications, until the
present day. *

God, we ultimately reach the substitution of Con-

disappearance of a supernatural Power. This de-
